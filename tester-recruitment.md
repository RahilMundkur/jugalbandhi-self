## Notes for you (not to send)

- **Tester count:** Google requires 12+ testers actively opted in for 14 continuous days. Aim for 15-20 to give yourself a cushion if a few drop off.
- **Opt-in URL location:** Play Console → Test and release → Testing → Closed testing → Testers tab → "Copy link"
- **Tester email list:** Add their Google account emails to your tester email list (same screen). They won't get an automatic email — you send them the link via your own channels.
- **Tracking who opted in:** Play Console shows opt-in counts but not individual statuses. If you're worried, ask testers to text you a screenshot after installing.
- **What constitutes "active":** As long as the app is installed and a tester opens it occasionally, they count. They don't need to use it heavily.
- **If you don't hit 12:** You can still test with fewer; the 14-day clock just won't count toward production access until you cross 12. Add more testers anytime.

## Optional: things you could ask testers to look at

Most testers won't dig deep, but if anyone offers, here are useful spots:

- **Cold start:** Does the app open quickly when you tap the icon?
- **Reading:** Try a chapter or two in English. Is the text comfortable to read?
- **Language switching:** Tap the EN pill at top-left, switch to another language (Hindi, Spanish, anything). Does the text translate properly?
- **Audio:** Switch to French/Hindi/Thai → tap Listen. The app offers to download the audio pack (~40 MB). Does that work? Does the audio play?
- **Bookmarks/notes:** Long-press a paragraph to highlight or add a note. Does it save?
- **Search:** Tap the magnifying-glass icon, type a word. Do results appear?
- **Dark mode:** Tap the moon icon top-right. Does the colour scheme switch cleanly?

Any "doesn't work" or "feels weird" report is gold. Don't worry about exhaustive coverage — you're just looking for surprises.
