## Short description

**Field**: Short description · **Limit**: 80 chars

**Text** (recommended for v3.1):

> Read & listen to a book on the Self in 35 languages. Offline reading.

*(70 / 80 chars)*

Why the change from your April draft: previous wording was "audio narration in 35 languages" which was never quite accurate (audio is only in EN/HI/FR/TH; the 35 languages are translations of the text). New wording separates the two — "read in 35 languages, listen in [the audio ones]" — without saying which audio languages, which keeps it short. Alternative variants if you want different emphasis:

- "A book on the Self in 35 languages with native audio narration." (60 chars — shorter, less specific)
- "Read in 35 languages. Listen in 4. Offline. Ad-free. No accounts." (66 chars — punchier, leans on offline/ad-free)
- "The Self, in 35 languages: read offline, listen in English & more." (66 chars — keeps the subtitle vibe)

Pick whichever fits your voice best. I'd recommend the one at the top.
