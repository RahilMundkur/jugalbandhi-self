# Release Notes — v3.3.0 (versionCode 78)

## Google Play "What's new" (433 characters, under the 500-char limit)

This update brings a comprehensive translation-quality pass across all 34 languages, correcting titles, terminology, and grammatical consistency throughout every chapter. We fixed an audio pronunciation bug and regenerated narration for all seven audio-pack languages (English, French, Hindi, Spanish, Chinese Simplified & Traditional, Thai). We also polished text rendering (footnotes, punctuation) for a cleaner reading experience.

## Longer-form notes (for a blog post, email, or App Store "What's New" if a longer limit applies)

**Translation quality overhaul.** Every one of the 34 non-English translations has been closely proofread against the English source and corrected for mistranslated chapter titles, false-friend word errors, gender-agreement mistakes, and inconsistent formal/informal address (a issue found and fixed at scale in Urdu, Chinese Traditional, Italian, Marathi, Burmese, and Vietnamese).

**Audio fixes.** Fixed a bug where a dash in the source text was occasionally read aloud as the word "to" instead of a natural pause. Narration has been regenerated for all seven audio-pack languages (English, French, Hindi, Spanish, Chinese Simplified, Chinese Traditional, Thai) to reflect the corrected translations.

**Reading experience.** Fixed a footnote marker that wasn't displaying as a superscript, and cleaned up stray straight quotation marks to match the book's typography throughout.
